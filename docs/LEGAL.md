# Legal and trust notes

ShowScout is a comparison and referral layer. It does not sell tickets, hold seats, or proxy booking-provider pages. Outbound booking links must use `rel="noopener noreferrer sponsored"` where affiliate economics apply, with a clear disclosure.

OSM data requires OpenStreetMap attribution and is subject to the ODbL. TMDB metadata and images require TMDB attribution and must be used under the current TMDB terms. Provider terms always take priority over ingestion convenience.

Demo data is clearly labelled. Operator and community reports are labelled by provenance and freshness; community reports are not presented as live inventory.
